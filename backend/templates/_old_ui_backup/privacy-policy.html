Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 1, 2024

1. Information We Collect

At Khushboo Jewellers, we collect information that you provide directly to us when you:

  • Contact us for wholesale partnership inquiries
  • Submit business information through our contact forms
  • Communicate with us via email or phone
  • Visit our showroom in Nayasadak, Cuttack

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries about our wholesale jewellery products
  • Provide information about our 925 sterling silver collections
  • Communicate about business partnerships and orders
  • Process wholesale orders and maintain business records
  • Improve our services and customer experience
  • Send updates about new collections and wholesale offers (with consent)

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations and regulations
  • To protect our rights, property, and business operations
  • With trusted service providers who assist in our business operations

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. We do not use third-party tracking for advertising purposes.
