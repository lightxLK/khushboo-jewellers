Common Questions

Wholesale Partnership FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for wholesale?

Our minimum order quantity varies by product category. For new partners, we recommend starting with a sample order to test the market. Contact us for specific MOQ details for your preferred product range.

Do you provide credit terms to retail partners?

Yes, we offer flexible credit terms to established retail partners after verification. New partners typically start with advance payment, and credit facilities are extended based on business relationship and transaction history.

How long does delivery take across India?

Within Odisha: 2-3 business days. Pan-India: 5-7 business days. We use reliable courier services with tracking. Express delivery options are available for urgent orders at additional cost.

Can I get custom designs for my retail store?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom design development based on your regional market preferences. Our design team works closely with you to create exclusive collections. Minimum order quantities apply for custom designs.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), UPI payments, cheques, and cash for local transactions. For bulk orders, we recommend bank transfers for proper documentation and GST compliance.

Is all your jewelry BIS hallmarked?

Yes, every piece we manufacture is BIS hallmarked 925 sterling silver. We provide proper certification and documentation with all orders, ensuring complete compliance for your retail business.
