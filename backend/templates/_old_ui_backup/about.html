Our Journey

The Khushboo Legacy

Founded in 2002 in the heart of Nayasadak, Cuttack, Khushboo Jewellers began as a small family workshop dedicated to preserving the ancient art of Odia silver craftsmanship.

What started with a single artisan's dream has blossomed into Odisha's most trusted wholesale silver jewelry partner, serving over 500 retailers across the state and beyond.

Our journey is rooted in the rich cultural heritage of Cuttack, renowned for centuries as the "Silver City of India." Every piece we craft carries the soul of traditional Odia artistry, combined with modern quality standards that retailers trust.
